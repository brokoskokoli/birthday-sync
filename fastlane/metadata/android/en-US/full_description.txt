Birthday Sync puts the birthdays from your contacts into the calendar you actually use – automatically and without any account or cloud service.

Choose which contact accounts or groups to include and which calendar the events should go to. The app creates an event for every birthday and keeps it up to date: if you change a name or a birth date, or remove a contact, the calendar follows at the next sync.

Features:
• Reads birthdays from the contact accounts and groups/labels you choose
• Writes them as events into a calendar of your choice, or into its own “Birthdays” calendar on the device
• All-day or at a set time, with an optional calendar reminder
• Detects changed birthdays and updates or removes the events
• Daily notification for today’s birthdays, at a time you choose
• Guided setup that explains every permission and helps with battery and background settings
• Event title with placeholders, e.g. “🎂 {name} ({age})”

Privacy:
• No internet access – your data never leaves the device
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Free and open source
